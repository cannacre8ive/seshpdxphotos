      about

      seshpdx is Spencer Gray's live-music photography, shot from the photo pit. Most of it is from Portland's small rooms, Holocene, the Doug Fir, Wonder Ballroom and the Crystal, and often from an artist's first headline run.

      The sheet is built from the full-resolution files: the finished edits first, then the raws, matched to each night by the camera's capture time. The index was rebuilt from folder names, keywords, capture times, GPS and signage in the frames, then checked against setlist archives, venue listings and press. Where a show couldn't be confirmed, the entry says so.
